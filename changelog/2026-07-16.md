## Trending States

- Added a new page, `trending-states.html`, that ranks all 50 states + DC (plus Maine's and Nebraska's congressional districts) by their recent political trajectory
- Two independent trend metrics computed client-side from recent presidential elections: a directional trend (shifting Democratic or Republican, relative to the national environment) and a competitiveness trend (margins narrowing or widening)
- Four categories: Trending Democratic, Trending Republican, Emerging Battlegrounds (becoming more competitive), and Solidifying States (becoming less competitive) — a state can appear in more than one
- Visualizations: a quadrant scatter plot (direction vs. competitiveness), a US map colored by trend with a Direction/Competitiveness toggle (including the ME/NE congressional district overlay), and ranked list panels with inline sparklines for each category
- The number of recent elections used for the trend window is adjustable (3–8 elections back, defaulting to the last 5)
- Updated header to include a link to the Trending States page

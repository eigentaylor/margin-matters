## Sim2028

- Rebuilt how monthly polls are generated. Each poll used to be a scalar D-R margin nudged by noise; it's now a genuine simulated poll - a state's (and separately, the nation's) hidden truth is expressed as a D/R/(third-party) vote-share point, an Undecided share is mixed in and shrinks across the campaign toward a small nonzero Election-Eve floor (nobody's poll is ever perfectly clean, even on the eve of the election), and 1,000 respondents are actually sampled from the resulting simplex every step. The one-time "mirage bias" that can make a state read the wrong way early in the campaign is still there, regionally correlated the same way it always was - it's the sampling noise on top that's new, replacing a flat statistical wobble with an actual poll of simulated people.
- The state polling table and the map hover tooltip now show the real D/R/(Third party)/Undecided percentage breakdown a poll like that would report (e.g. "47% D · 44% R · 9% U"), with the projected margin kept underneath as a smaller reference line. Previously both only ever showed the margin.
- This is purely a poll-generation change - the forecast model, the electoral snake, and election night's own vote synthesis are untouched and still consume the same relative-margin/national-popular-vote numbers as before.

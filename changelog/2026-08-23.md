## Election Night

- Album captions (the text under each image in the "Election Night Album" grid) now render in full caps, and no longer say a state was "called" - a normal call now reads "STATE PROJECTED FOR CANDIDATE," reserving "CANDIDATE WINS STATE" for the rare case where the remaining uncounted votes in that state can no longer mathematically overcome the leader's margin. A lead-change caption now reads "CANDIDATE TAKES THE LEAD IN STATE."
- Replaced the hardcoded list of seven states (WI/MI/PA/AZ/GA/NV/NC) that were always treated as 2028 "key races" regardless of how competitive a given simulated run actually made them. A state now counts as a key race if its pre-election rating is Toss-up or Tilt D/R, or if its true simulated margin is itself within a Toss-up-sized margin of even - so a landslide run no longer force-highlights states that weren't actually close.
- The "takes the lead" checkpoint card (live sim and album) now shows a full D/R vote-total comparison box - raw counts, share of total, and how many votes the new leader is ahead by - matching what the state call card already shows. A third-party row now also appears on it under the same conditions it would appear on a call screen (a real 2028 sim run with third party enabled, or a historical year with a genuine third-party showing like 1968/1992/1996).

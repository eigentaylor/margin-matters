## Election Night

- Album captions (the text under each image in the "Election Night Album" grid) now render in full caps, and no longer say a state was "called" - a normal call now reads "STATE PROJECTED FOR CANDIDATE," reserving "CANDIDATE WINS STATE" for the rare case where the remaining uncounted votes in that state can no longer mathematically overcome the leader's margin. A lead-change caption now reads "CANDIDATE TAKES THE LEAD IN STATE."
- Replaced the hardcoded list of seven states (WI/MI/PA/AZ/GA/NV/NC) that were always treated as 2028 "key races" regardless of how competitive a given simulated run actually made them. A state now counts as a key race if its pre-election rating is Toss-up or Tilt D/R, or if its true simulated margin is itself within a Toss-up-sized margin of even - so a landslide run no longer force-highlights states that weren't actually close.
- The "takes the lead" checkpoint card (live sim and album) now shows a full D/R vote-total comparison box - raw counts, share of total, and how many votes the new leader is ahead by - matching what the state call card already shows. A third-party row now also appears on it under the same conditions it would appear on a call screen (a real 2028 sim run with third party enabled, or a historical year with a genuine third-party showing like 1968/1992/1996).

## Sim2028

- Fixed the third-party candidate feature (added yesterday, still beta) so its numbers actually reach the screen. The debug panel's true-result tally was silently folding a third-party win into D or R instead of showing it as its own outcome - it's now a single, unified three-way tally that always agrees with the rest of the debug panel.
- The map's popular-vote badges had a hardcoded "Other" row that never displayed, even with a real third-party vote share flowing through - it now appears (with its own vote total and percentage) whenever the third party is drawing any votes, and stays hidden otherwise.
- Fixed a state the third party won sometimes rendering in the wrong color (blue instead of gold).
- The post-election "album" summary had its own separate, two-party-only winner check that would mislabel a third-party win as a Republican win. It now recognizes a third-party winner the same way the rest of the app already does.
- Added a "(beta)" note to the Third party setting, since the mechanic itself (not just this round's display bugs) may still need more tuning.
- The state-call popup's third-party comparison row was still gated behind a 5% floor meant for historical noise-filtering (a real Libertarian/Green showing shouldn't clutter every call) - but for a real sim2028 run, any showing the setting produces is worth seeing, so that floor no longer applies when third party is actually running in this simulator (historical replays keep the original 5% floor).

## General

- Began calculating state "elasticity" for presidential elections, defined as the ratio of a state's raw presidential margin change to the change in national popular vote margin.
- You can view these in the ranker and main pages now.
  - In the ranker, elasticity is a metric you can choose
  - In the main page, elasticity is shown at the bottom with the other deltas
- The dataset now has these elasticity values included for each state and year where applicable.

## Flip scenarios

- Added a "Dim non-flipped states" toggle to the flip scenario controls. With it on, applying a flip (winner flip, majority break, or tie) fades every state that wasn't part of the scenario into the background and highlights the ones that were with a glowing outline, so it's immediately clear which states actually moved - previously every state looked the same regardless of whether it was involved.
- That dim-and-highlight sequence now animates smoothly and in a deliberate order instead of snapping on instantly and all at once: the non-flipped states fade out first, and the flipped states' glow appears alongside that fade - even before their color visibly changes - so a small or easy-to-miss flip (like Hawaii) is still obvious the moment you click. The flipped states' colors and glow color then settle a beat later once the actual result renders. This also fixed Maine and Nebraska's split-district shapes, which previously had no fade transition of their own.
- Added `tools/generate_flip_gif.mjs`, a script that drives the live flip-scenario page headlessly and records an animated GIF of the whole sequence (map recoloring, EV bar shifting, the dim/glow effect) for use as a blog-post illustration, instead of needing to screen-record it by hand. It supports an experimental `--rendervotes` overlay that labels each flipped state with its exact vote margin, plus the national total above the EV bar, so a reader can see at a glance just how few votes it actually took to flip a given election.

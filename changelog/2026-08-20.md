## Election Night

- The very first checkpoint of election night (the KY/IN poll-close batch) now opens with a "portrait of the race" title card - the election name plus both major-party candidates' photos, and a smaller photo for the major third-party candidate too in years with a real electoral-vote haul for one (e.g. 1968, 1992, 1996). Shown in both the live simulator and the Election Night Album.
- In the sim2028 simulator specifically, that opening title card also shows a small stats row: projected win probability for each candidate (capped at 99.9% either way), the probability of an electoral college tie, an estimated national popular vote margin from the starting polls, and a median electoral vote estimate.

## Election Night

- The very first checkpoint of election night (the KY/IN poll-close batch) now opens with a "portrait of the race" title card - the election name, each major-party candidate's full name and photo in a party-colored border, and a smaller photo for the major third-party candidate too in years with a real electoral-vote haul for one (e.g. 1968, 1992, 1996). Shown in both the live simulator and the Election Night Album.
- In the sim2028 simulator specifically, that opening title card also shows a small stats row: projected win probability for each candidate (capped at 99.9% either way), the probability of an electoral college tie, an estimated national popular vote margin from the starting polls, and a median electoral vote estimate.
- Each candidate's photo border hugs the actual portrait instead of a generic square, so a non-square photo doesn't end up with empty letterboxed corners inside the border.
- The opening title card's win %/tie %/median-EV/NPV stats (sim2028 only) now come from the campaign's own Election-Eve forecast - the same estimate shown on the sim2028 forecast panel - instead of the live election-night win-probability model, which is deliberately hedged close to 50-50 in the earliest minutes of the count since it hasn't seen any actual returns yet.
- The default election-night playback speed is now 1.0x instead of 0.5x, on both the historical simulator and sim2028.
- The final "Key Races" recap no longer ends on a sparse last page (e.g. just one or two cards): it now fills any partial final page out to a full 6 with the next-closest remaining races by margin, so a checkpoint that would've spanned 3 pages shows 18 races instead of trailing off after 13.
- The mid-night "Races to watch" slide can now span up to 2 pages (12 races) instead of always capping at 6, so a busy stretch of the night with many uncalled/key races isn't truncated - it still shows just 1 page when there aren't enough races to fill a second.

## Aleck Lickman (v0.1)

- Added a first pass at pundit commentary for election night: Aleck Lickman, a parody of Allan Lichtman, predicts the winner using his "13 Beets to the Presidency" system, then reacts once the final result is in.
- Right after the opening title card, Lickman announces how many of his 13 beets came back false and confidently calls a winner by name. For any real election (every year the 13 Keys page covers), he uses the actual graded false-key count directly, so his historical track record matches the real Lichtman system's exactly - including its famous hits (2000, 2016, 2020) and its 2024 miss. Only the fictional 2028 race has no real keys to grade, so there he estimates the false-beet count from the true national popular vote instead, via the same least-squares fit the 13 Keys page uses (false keys vs. incumbent-relative NPV margin), inverted and run through a seeded margin of error - so he can be confidently wrong there too, same as the real Keys.
- After the final tally (and any key-race recap), Lickman gives a closing reaction - one of four flavors depending on whether his prediction matched the popular-vote winner, the electoral-college winner, both, or neither.
- Shown in both the live simulator and the (headless) Election Night Album. Optional and on by default via a new "Show Aleck Lickman commentary" toggle next to "Show state-call updates," on the historical simulator and sim2028 alike.
- The 2028 estimate's wobble is seeded off whatever's actually driving that run's randomness (sim2028's own campaign seed, or the historical page's random-PV seed, or just the year when nothing else is randomized), so replaying the same setup calls the same beet count instead of re-rolling on every Start/Reset.
- Mid-night reactive commentary (as calls move toward/away from his prediction) isn't wired up yet, but the dialogue for it has been drafted for later.
- `?debug=true` on the sim2028 page now also surfaces Lickman's false-beet count, and (for the 2028 estimate specifically) the underlying regression fit.

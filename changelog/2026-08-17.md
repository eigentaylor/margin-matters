## Election Night

- The playback footer (Pause/Resume, Reset, speed, progress) now stays visible and clickable during a state-call popup instead of being covered by it
- Pause during a state call now freezes that call on its current slide (auto-advance stops) while still letting you click, tap, or press space/enter/arrow-left to manually step through the remaining slides; the sim clock stays paused afterward until you press Resume
- Reset now works mid-state-call, immediately closing the popup and exiting election night mode, same as it already did between calls
- "Elected"/"Reelected" wording is now reserved for the night's true final tally; every earlier clinch or corrected-call moment reads as "Projected to be elected/reelected" instead, since the winner isn't settled yet at that point
- Fixed the state-call popup's electoral vote scoreboard, which could show incorrect totals in a few cases: two states called at the same moment (e.g. 2000's Georgia and South Carolina), a state's later correction, and split-elector states like 1960 Alabama/Mississippi; the whole night's tally is now precomputed up front instead of recalculated live, so it's no longer sensitive to playback speed
- The scoreboard's "+N"/"-N" delta chip now appears for every call, not just corrections, so you can see at a glance how many electoral votes a state just added
- Delta chips now fade out smoothly instead of disappearing abruptly, and read more clearly against their background
- Fixed a bug where, if the last state call/correction of the night locked in an Electoral College tie (or otherwise knocked the leader below a majority) at the exact same moment as the night's true final tally, the popup could get stuck on a "No longer decided" breaking-news screen and never reach the real final "Decided by the House of Representatives" screen
- A called state can now be retracted ("too close to call") mid-count if its confidence collapses well before it finishes reporting, then called again later once it recovers - not just corrected at the very end, matching how Florida was called for Gore in 2000 and then explicitly un-called. Retracted calls stay in the call log alongside the eventual re-call, the map/tooltip/small-box coloring reverts to in-progress while a state is retracted, and the state-call scoreboard/checkpoint popups account for it so electoral votes are never double-counted
- States whose true winner matches the eventual Electoral College winner are now somewhat more likely to show a fake early lean toward the eventual loser before correcting, and states whose true winner is the eventual loser are somewhat less likely to falsely lean toward the winner - the same "wrong candidate leads early" drama, just tilted to happen more often on the side of the eventual loser rather than padding the eventual winner's early lead
- A retracted call now gets its own popup screen (previously it fell through to a broken "WINNER" card with a placeholder avatar) - a distinct amber-red "too close to call" screen showing who was previously called
- Fake early leads now swing bigger for states already fake-leaning toward the wrong candidate, and a retracted state now stays "too close to call" for at least 30 simulated minutes before it can be called again, instead of potentially flipping back right away

## 2028 Campaign Simulator

- Added a "Candidates" picker to the Setup panel: pick a preset D or R candidate (Beshear, Buttigieg, Ocasio-Cortez, Newsom, Ossoff for D; Rubio, Vance for R), or go "Custom…" to type any name and optionally upload your own portrait
- A custom portrait can now be dragged and dropped anywhere onto a candidate's box, not just picked via the file button; the native file input's own "No file chosen" label is a browser security limit and will never reflect drag-dropped or reloaded images, but a live thumbnail preview next to it shows the actual state, plus a "Remove image" button to clear it
- Fixed an uploaded custom portrait not surviving a page reload
- Candidate picks are display-only (call-log popups, map tooltips) and have no effect on the simulated result, matching how the model has always worked
- Preset and custom-name picks are saved to this browser and also round-trip through "Share URL"; an uploaded custom portrait is saved to this browser only

## 2028 Simulator

- Added the 2028 Simulator to the site header nav, linking to `sim2028.html`
- Bumped the in-page version notice from "Prototype (v0.1)" to "Beta (v0.9)" and updated the note to reflect that it's now linked from the nav (still tunable and not yet fully calibrated)
- Added a candidate search box to the Democrat/Republican picker: type any name (or year) to pull up every historical portrait on file back to 1864 - Reagan, FDR, Tilden, Perot, and everyone else - and drop them into either party's slot with no restriction on their real historical affiliation
- Candidate-year pairs that reuse the exact same portrait photo (e.g. the 1992/1996 Perot photo, or Cleveland's 1884/1888 one) now collapse to a single search result at the earlier year, since there's nothing visually distinct to pick between

## Election Night: Album

- The Election Night Album now generates its own, much tighter checkpoint schedule instead of reusing the live sim's - the live popup is tuned to minimize interruptions, but the album is a static image set with nobody to interrupt, so it now paces out into noticeably more, smaller "photos" across the night
- Fixed poll-close cards sometimes displaying out of chronological order - a "Polls just closed" card could previously get pulled behind a later breaking call within the same batch (e.g. Alaska/Hawaii's 1am close showing up after a Pennsylvania call that happened 18 minutes later); poll-close now always forces a clean chapter break at its true moment
- Breaking call/correction/retraction/lead-change cards now show the "Breaking news" ribbon at the top of the image, matching the live popup - previously only the outcome/final-tally cards got the ribbon
- "Races to watch" cards are now paced independently instead of tagging along with every checkpoint: they show up during a genuine lull in the action (so a tense race's margin narrowing between calls is actually visible) and right before a chapter break like a poll-close, rather than after nearly every single call
- Fixed the map snapshot right after a "Polls just closed" card looking identical to the one before it (nothing new has counted yet at the exact instant polls close) - it now waits until partway to the next checkpoint before capturing, so there's actually something new to see, without ever showing a call before its own card has announced it
- Fixed map snapshots barely changing between consecutive images - with the album's tighter checkpoint schedule, a map was still being captured at every single checkpoint (some only minutes apart), which just produced near-duplicates; map snapshots now pace independently the same way "Races to watch" cards do, capturing during a real lull or at a chapter boundary rather than at every checkpoint
- "Races to watch" cards now show a timestamp, both on the image and in its caption, like every other card kind already did

## Election Night

- Fixed several places that decide whether a call/correction/retraction/lead-change counts as "breaking news" (which controls front-of-batch ordering and the breaking-news flash) not consistently honoring the 2028 simulator's forced perennial-battleground list (Arizona, Georgia, Wisconsin, Nevada, Michigan, Pennsylvania, North Carolina) - a sim where one of these states' closeness score happened to land below the normal "key race" bar could previously show it out of order behind less important races
- Simultaneous calls that tie in time now break ties by electoral vote count (bigger state first) when nothing else distinguishes them, instead of an arbitrary order

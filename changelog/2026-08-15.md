## Election Night

- Added a live national win-probability estimate that updates throughout the night as states report, based on a statistical model of each state's remaining uncertainty
- The estimate accounts for a shared national mood, not just each state's own results in isolation: as states report, it infers a shared national swing and regional swings (New England, Rust Belt, South, etc.) from how those states are over- or under-performing their pre-election expectations
- Tuned the pacing of the estimate to feel like an actual election night rather than a spreadsheet: it starts more uncertain and narrows gradually as real results come in, instead of jumping to near-certainty the moment a handful of states report
- Reworked the call log into a dedicated, full-height sidebar on wide screens, colorized by leader, showing the national popular vote and each call's raw vote margin alongside the standard percentage
- Added a downloadable call-log export (TXT/CSV) covering the full night, including a timeline of the model's national and regional swing estimates
- The displayed confidence percentage now scales against whatever call threshold you've set, instead of always assuming the default — set a stricter or looser threshold and the "how sure are we" reading adjusts to match
- The declared national outcome ("X clinches the presidency") now only reflects results that have actually been made public; if a correction to a state's call changes who's actually projected to win, that gets its own explicit log entry instead of the banner silently changing
- Fixed vote counting on very close races: it used to stall near 100% for a stretch and then jump straight to the final tally once a state finished reporting; counting now proceeds smoothly all the way through
- Fixed the reporting percentage showing "100.0% counted" while ballots were technically still left to count; it now only shows 100% once every ballot is actually in
- The map's hover tooltips now show the same confidence and reporting figures as the call log, instead of a rougher, less consistent version
- Added a dedicated playback footer (play/pause, reset, speed, and the progress scrubber) that appears at the bottom of the screen during election night mode, so the controls stay reachable without scrolling back up
- Smaller touches: candidate last names in call announcements, a year label in the call log header, free-text speed input instead of a dropdown, and a corrected confidence readout for exact ties

## Election Night

- Added a live national win-probability estimate that updates throughout the night as states report, based on a Bayesian model of each state's remaining uncertainty
- The estimate now accounts for a shared national swing, not just each state's own results in isolation — a Monte Carlo bug meant the national mood was previously assumed to be known with total certainty, which made the early estimate overconfident
- Built a hierarchical "live swing" model: as states report, it infers a shared national swing and regional swings (New England, Rust Belt, South, etc.) from how those states are over/underperforming their pre-election priors — the foundation for a future "Clinton is underperforming in the Rust Belt" style narrative feature
- Tuned the pacing of the win-probability estimate to feel less like a spreadsheet and more like an actual election night: it now starts more uncertain and narrows more gradually as real results come in, rather than converging to near-certainty the moment a handful of states report
- Removed the per-state win% from "still counting" cards — it could read as suspiciously precise for a race that's genuinely a toss-up; the existing confidence/margin info already tells that story
- Fixed the win-probability estimate getting stuck on a state's original call and not updating after that call was later corrected
- Fixed the EV allocation arrow revealing a state's corrected result before the correction was actually made
- Fixed vote counting on very close races: it used to freeze near 100% for a stretch and then jump straight to the final result once the state finished reporting; counting now proceeds smoothly all the way to completion
- Fixed the reporting percentage showing "100.0% counted" while ballots were still left to count; it only shows 100% once every ballot is in
- The map's hover tooltips now show the same rescaled, easier-to-read confidence percentage as the call log, instead of a much shakier-looking raw value
- Reworked the call log panel: it's a dedicated full-height sidebar on wide screens, colorized by leader, and includes the national popular vote and vote-margin figures alongside each call
- Added a downloadable call-log export (txt/csv) with a full timeline of the model's swing estimates, useful for reviewing how a given night's results unfolded
- Various smaller fixes: candidate last names in call announcements, a year label in the call log header, free-text speed input instead of a dropdown, and a fixed confidence display for exact ties

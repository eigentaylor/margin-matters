## Sim2028

- Split the campaign map's polling tooltip so each candidate gets its own row instead of cramming the whole D/R/(third-party)/Undecided share breakdown and the derived margin onto one crowded "Poll" line. The derived margin now sits in its own row right below the 2020/2024 history rows, followed by a row per candidate (using their actual last names when a pick is active) and an Undecided row.
- Fixed that same tooltip's "Poll" margin row to measure whoever's actually leading against the real runner-up instead of always reporting the D-vs-R gap - a state where a third-party candidate was genuinely the frontrunner or runner-up was showing a misleading margin (e.g. "D+9.9") instead of the real race, even though the rating badge right above it ("Lean O") already had it right. That row now labels the leader by their last name (e.g. "Vance+12.6", "Perot+8.2") rather than the D/R/O letter used elsewhere in the app.
- Aleck Lickman now reacts a third time on election night, right after whichever comes first - the national popular vote getting called, or a candidate clinching the presidency (270+ electoral votes) - with dialogue that either claims credit or moves the goalposts depending on whether that call matched his prediction and how the still-uncalled race is currently trending. Appears in both the live simulation and the "Election Night Album" export.

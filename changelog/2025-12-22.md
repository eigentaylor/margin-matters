## Election Night

- Fixed a bug where the election night panel was accidentally hidden by styling rules meant for diagnostics panels.
- Fixed a bug where election night would say you needed 270 electoral votes to win, even if you were simulating a year where fewer electoral votes were needed.
- Added "phantom" bars to the election night EV bar to indicate how many electoral votes each party would have if all uncalled states (for which that party is currently leading) were awarded to that party.

## Election Night

- Fixed a national-popular-vote accounting bug in election night for pre-district-split Maine/Nebraska years (for example 1880): `ME-AL`/`NE-AL` rows are now treated as normal statewide vote contributors before the split years, so the end-of-night NPV margin/count no longer drifts to an incorrect D lead.
- Added a checkpoint popup that periodically interrupts the night to recap what's happened: new calls, corrections, and a running scoreboard, batched together instead of firing one at a time for every single state
- The scoreboard box in the popup shows a live electoral vote tally for each party with an animated count-up, plus each party's live win probability
- Added a genuine tie/no-majority probability alongside the D and R win percentages ("D 48.2% — R 46.5% — Tie 5.3%"), so a contingent-election scenario can actually be represented instead of forcing a binary D-or-R read. The tie percentage now reads an honest 0.0% once a no-majority result becomes mathematically provable, rather than being stuck at a token nonzero floor
- The election is now called as a no-majority/contingent election as soon as it becomes mathematically impossible for either party to reach 270, instead of waiting for every last state to be called
- Years with a serious third-party electoral vote haul (1968, 1948, 1912, 1860, etc.) now show that party in the persistent running EV tally, not just D and R
- Third-party leads now show a real computed margin ("O+13.1", with the matching raw vote count underneath) everywhere a D/R margin would normally appear — call log, races-to-watch, and checkpoint slides — instead of a generic "Other lead" placeholder
- Added a "races to watch" slide highlighting the closest uncalled contests, and a dedicated slide for the final result once the night wraps up
- Uncropped candidate portraits on the "X ELECTED PRESIDENT" slide so the full portrait shows instead of a cropped circle
- Lowered the default call-confidence threshold from 0.5 to 0.15, making calls come in earlier by default
- Fixed the night sometimes ending before a razor-thin national popular vote race (e.g. 1880) ever got officially called
- Fixed the 1948 "flip to no-majority" scenario not actually working, caused by an Alabama data quirk (Thurmond electors recorded under the Democratic line) that was letting Truman keep votes he shouldn't have had
- Fixed checkpoint scheduling so results don't jump around or rewind, and so the final recap slide reliably shows up at the end of the night instead of occasionally getting stranded
- Fixed the sim2028 election-night button swapping in the election-night footer immediately on click, which broke navigating back through the campaign timeline before pressing Start; the footer now only switches once election night actually begins, and switches back correctly on Reset
- Winner announcements (checkpoint popup and call log) now say which numbered presidency is on the line — "Elected 47th President" for a new president, or "Reelected as 47th President" when an incumbent (or a former president winning a non-consecutive term, like Cleveland 1892, T. Roosevelt 1912, or Trump 2024) wins another term. Applies to the main election-night and sim2028 simulations only; future.html's hypothetical years are unaffected
- Fixed a same-term incumbent's reelection ordinal being off by one (e.g. Coolidge showing as "reelected as 31st president" instead of the 30th president he actually was) — a reelected incumbent now correctly keeps their existing number instead of the next one

Data Sources & Attribution

This project uses U.S. presidential election results data from Wikipedia articles, scraped and processed into maps and visualizations.

Sources

  • Wikipedia articles: Category:United States presidential elections by date and individual election year pages (1864–2024).
  • License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • Modifications: Data scraped, cleaned, and transformed into structured datasets and map visualizations.

Original article histories and contributors are available at each Wikipedia page.

Acknowledgments

This site would not be possible without the contributions of thousands of Wikipedia editors who maintain accurate historical election data. We are grateful for their work and the open licensing that enables projects like this.

Electoral College Tipping Point

The tipping point is the state that gives the winning candidate their 270th electoral vote. It represents the decisive margin that determines the electoral college outcome.

Understanding the chart:
• When the tipping point is positive (above 0), Republicans are advantaged - they can win the Electoral College while losing the popular vote
• When the tipping point is negative (below 0), Democrats are advantaged - they can win the Electoral College while losing the popular vote
• The colored area between the tipping point line and zero shows which party has the structural advantage
• The difference view shows how much the popular vote differs from the tipping point margin
